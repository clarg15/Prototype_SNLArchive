The idea behind the archive

About SNL Archive

A fan-made concept for bringing Saturday Night Live memories and conversations together.

Made for the fans who keep watching.

SNL Archive brings Saturday Night Live fans together in one place to discuss and rate sketches, rank favorites, and explore facts about the show.

Our goal is to make it easier for fans to discover more about SNL and share what they love about it.
