## 4. Can it be copied? (the moat question)

Anyone in this class could rebuild your website in a weekend with the same AI tool. So the code is **not** your competitive advantage.

- Valuable - saves fans time by providing detailed SNL information in one palce. Rare - a comprehensive, organized database with this level of detail is hard to find. Hard to imitate - competitors would need to spend a lot of time and research to document thousnads of sketches and performances in order to accurately present them to users. Non-substitutable - no easy replacement for one dedicated platorm that compiles all SNL information into one searchable database. Fans would otherwise need to use mulitple sources.
- A competitor witha large budget could recreate the website itself fairly quickly, but compiling a detailed and accurate database could take months or even years of research and organization. 
- The platform gets stronger over time as there are more fan ratings, reviews and discussions, making the platfomr increasingly difficult to replicate.
